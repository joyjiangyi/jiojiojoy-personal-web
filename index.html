Content & Audience Growth海外官方账号
内容与用户增长

Promote is TikTok’s in-app paid promotion tool for creators and small businesses. This project operates four official channels, using localized tutorials and customer stories to build product awareness, grow followers and help people get started.Promote 是 TikTok 内的付费推广工具，帮助创作者和中小商家推广内容。这个项目运营四个海外官方账号，用本地化教学和案例讲清产品价值，吸引目标用户关注，并引导他们开始使用。

Project Results项目成果

The four-channel portfolio reached 9.65 million followers, gaining 5.60 million overall, including 4.29 million on the English-language channel (November 6, 2025–September 18, 2026). Paid follower share increased from 15.32% to 18.37%, up 3.05 percentage points (January 3–June 29, 2026).四个官方账号总粉丝达 964.85 万，累计净增 559.85 万，其中英语主账号净增 429.05 万（2025.11.06—2026.09.18）。付费粉丝占比从 15.32% 提升至 18.37%，增长 3.05 个百分点（2026.01.03—06.29）。

Content Strategy & Operations内容策略与运营体系

Built around localized content strategy, audience segmentation and coordinated distribution, the project connects product education, brand communication and practical use cases. Performance analysis and multilingual audience feedback inform ongoing content planning and optimization across markets.围绕本地化内容策略、用户分层与协同分发，构建涵盖产品教育、品牌传播和实操案例的内容体系；结合数据分析与多语言用户反馈，形成从内容规划、制作发布到效果复盘的跨市场运营闭环。

Advertising creative for TikTok Promote across 19 countries, reaching local creators and merchants. Live-action, animation, AI and repurposed footage are refined through campaign feedback to connect local relevance with conversion performance.为 TikTok Promote 制作面向 19 个国家的推广广告，吸引当地创作者与商家了解和使用产品。项目结合真人短视频、动画、AI 与二创素材，持续根据投放反馈调整创意，兼顾本地表达与转化效果。

Project Overview & Results项目内容与成果

TikTok Promote is an in-app advertising tool for creators and small businesses. This project develops localized video ads that explain when to promote, show how the product works and encourage viewers to try it. Creative is adapted to the language, content preferences and usage scenarios of 19 markets across Asia-Pacific, Europe, the Middle East, Latin America and the US.TikTok Promote 是面向创作者和中小商家的站内推广工具。本项目为其制作海外效果广告，把产品功能转化为用户能理解的使用场景，引导目标人群了解并尝试推广。内容覆盖亚太、欧洲、中东、拉美及美国等 19 个市场，结合当地语言、内容偏好和商家需求进行创意适配。

The project delivered 3,610 creative assets with a weighted average CPA of $12.91 (Q2–Q4 2025). Live-action, animation, AI-generated creative and repurposed footage provide different ways to test the product message across audiences.项目累计交付 3,610 条创意素材，加权平均单次转化成本（CPA）为 $12.91（2025 Q2–Q4）。通过真人短视频、动画、AIGC 与二创等多种形式，持续测试适合不同市场的产品表达。
